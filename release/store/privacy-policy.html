Mtouaa Studio — Privacy policy Prepared 3 November 2026 Publisher: Mtouaa Labs Website: https://mtouaa.com Contact: [Support email must be added before publication] Your documents Documents, images, audio and other files you open are processed on your device. The app does not upload your documents to the publisher or an AI service. Android's document picker grants access only to files you select. If you choose a cloud-storage provider in that picker, that provider handles storage under its own terms. Accounts, advertising and analytics The app does not require an account. The publisher has not added advertising, analytics, tracking or payment SDKs. The bundled Mozilla browser runtime executes the editors locally. Its open-source notices and corresponding source are available in About & licenses. Network access The editors load their files from an on-device loopback server; this traffic stays on your device. Editor web requests to outside sites are blocked. The Google Play edition does not check GitHub or download replacement editors. Google Play handles app distribution and updates under Google's policies. Opening Support or the public privacy page uses your browser, where the website's privacy practices apply. Local storage and deletion Editor preferences, browser storage and temporary import/export files may be stored locally. Selected documents are read through Android's granted document access and copied into private cache while the editor needs them. These import copies are removed when the editor closes or on the next app launch; originals are not changed. Temporary export files are cleared after use or on the next app launch. You choose where exported documents are saved. Clear the app's storage or uninstall it to remove its internal data; exported files remain where you saved them and can be deleted using your file manager. Permissions Internet and network-state permissions are needed by the local browser/server connection and browser runtime. The app does not request broad storage access, contacts, location, microphone or camera permissions. Android's file picker handles document access. Children This general-purpose creative suite is not specifically designed for children. The Play Console target-audience declaration must match the intended audience selected by the publisher. Changes and questions This policy will be updated when the app's data practices change. Contact the publisher at the address above with privacy questions.
