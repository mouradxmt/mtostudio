All editors remain bundled for offline use. Mtouaa branding, Android file importing, touch-menu support and home-screen shortcuts are retained. Original engine, dependency, font and language-data notices remain included.

**Huawei:** use the signed Huawei APK, or choose **Check for updates** from an existing Mtouaa Huawei installation. Google Play Services are not required. Save/export work before updating.

**Google Play:** the AAB is prepared for Play Console; this GitHub release does not publish it to Google Play. The Play APK uses Google Play update delivery when installed through Play.

The existing app IDs and signing key are unchanged. Preview is a separate app: export/import documents when moving from Preview.

AdMob is planned and is not included. Physical Huawei acceptance and pending Play publisher/privacy requirements remain outstanding.

Known font limitation: Vectors requests some optional Arabic font families that are absent from its upstream-pinned font archive; those additional families are not bundled.
