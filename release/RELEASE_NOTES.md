Mtouaa Studio 0.6.0 adds a **Check for updates** button on the Apps screen.

- **Huawei APK:** install this edition on Android-compatible Huawei tablets. Updates come from signed Mtouaa GitHub releases and use Android's installation confirmation. Google Play Services are not required for updates.
- **Google Play:** uses Google Play's in-app update flow after installation from a Play testing or production track. The AAB is for Play Console; a locally installed APK cannot exercise the real Play update service.
- All 15 editors remain bundled for offline use. Connecting to check/download updates is optional.

Save and export your work before installing an update. The Huawei edition has its own application ID; moving from Preview requires exporting documents in Preview and importing them in the Huawei edition. Keep Preview until your files are safely transferred.

AdMob is planned and is not included. Store submission still requires the pending support contact, hosted privacy policy, and Play declarations.
