# Mtouaa Studio artwork

The installed suite icon, all 15 editor shortcut icons and replacement engine brand images
are original geometric artwork created for Mtouaa Studio. They use an M-shaped open workspace
with a teal stroke, navy background and gold accent. No upstream logos, raven icons, traced
artwork or proprietary fonts are used.

Vector source and raster generator: `tools/prepare_play_sources.py`.
Android vector: `app/src/play/res/drawable/ic_launcher.xml`.
Store exports: `release/store/mark.svg`, `release/store/wordmark.svg`, `release/store/icon-512.png`.
