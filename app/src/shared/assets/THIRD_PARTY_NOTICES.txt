ArtCraft Offline is an independent Android port. It is not an official ArtCraft or Mozilla product.

The 15 bundled ArtCraft web engines retain their upstream MIT/Apache and asset/font licenses.
License, NOTICE, ATTRIBUTION and OFL files are preserved with each engine inside the app assets.
Source repositories are listed in the suite catalog and README.

separate system WebView installation is required by the default bundled browser engine.
